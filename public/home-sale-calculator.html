Selling your primary home? Enter what you paid and what you're selling for, and this calculator applies the IRS Section 121 exclusion first ($250,000 of gain tax-free if single, $500,000 married filing jointly, with the 2-of-5-year tests), then estimates federal capital gains tax, the 3.8% NIIT, and your state's tax on whatever is left. Example: a married couple with $80,000 of taxable income and a $650,000 gain excludes $500,000, leaving $150,000 taxable and roughly $19,665 of federal tax.
