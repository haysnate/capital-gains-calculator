About Capital Gains Tax Calculator

Capital Gains Tax Calculator estimates what you'll owe when you sell an investment — stocks, crypto, property, or your home. Enter your purchase and sale price, how long you held it, your filing status, taxable income, and state, and see your federal capital gains tax, the 3.8% net investment income tax, and an estimated state tax instantly.

Short-term vs. long-term, side by side

The single biggest lever on capital gains tax is your holding period. The calculator applies 2026 ordinary income brackets to short-term gains and the 0% / 15% / 20% long-term brackets to assets held over a year — and shows exactly how much holding past the one-year mark saves you.

Home sales handled

Selling your primary residence? One checkbox applies the IRS Section 121 exclusion — up to $250,000 of gain tax-free ($500,000 married filing jointly) — before any tax is calculated.

Private and free

Every calculation runs locally in your browser. Nothing you enter is uploaded, stored, or sent anywhere. No sign-up, nothing to install. For details on the cookies used by advertising, see our Privacy Policy.
